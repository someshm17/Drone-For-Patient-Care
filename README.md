# ⚖️ Digital Legal Marketplace

A simple and user-friendly full-stack web platform that connects people with the right lawyers and makes legal services easier to access, manage, and track—all in one place.

---

## 🚀 About the Project

Finding legal help can often be confusing, time-consuming, and expensive. This project aims to simplify that process by creating a **digital marketplace for legal services**.

It allows users to:

* Find verified lawyers based on their needs
* Book consultations online
* Upload and manage legal documents
* Track case progress in real-time
* Make secure digital payments

The goal is to make legal services more **accessible, transparent, and hassle-free**, especially for individuals and small businesses.

## ✨ Features

* 🔍 **Lawyer Search & Filtering** – Find lawyers based on specialization, location, and ratings
* 📅 **Online Consultation Booking** – Schedule appointments easily
* 📄 **Document Management** – Upload and access legal files securely
* 🤖 **Automated Document Generation** – Generate common legal documents
* 💳 **Secure Payments** – Smooth and safe transaction system
* 📊 **Case Tracking** – Monitor progress with real-time updates
* 🔐 **Authentication & Security** – Role-based login (Client / Lawyer / Admin)

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. User signs up / logs in
2. Searches for a lawyer based on requirements
3. Books a consultation
4. Uploads documents & communicates with the lawyer
5. Tracks case progress
6. Makes secure payments
